Mechanical mulching / fuel reduction

Lower wildfire fuel loads. Cleared ground around what you need to protect.

FECON Bull Hog mulching for wildland-urban interface work, timber thinning and clearing on public and private land.

Request a Quote
The outcome

Defensible space, thinned stands, usable ground.

Mechanical mulching turns brush and small timber into mulch in place. Fuel goes down, the soil is protected, and there’s no burning or hauling.

  • Reduced wildfire fuel loads around structures, communities and infrastructure.
  • Wildland-urban interface work where fire risk meets homes and facilities.
  • Timber thinning and clearing on public and private land.
  • Mulch left in place to protect soil and limit erosion.
The capability

We run what we sell.

SRI operates the FECON Bull Hog and is an authorized FECON dealer. We sell mulchers and forestry attachments and stock parts for FECON and Tigercat machines.

Equipment & parts
  • Fuel breaks and defensible space
  • Right-of-way and corridor clearing
  • Pinyon-juniper and brush removal for habitat projects
  • Pre-construction site clearing
