Hydroseeding

Fast, even coverage on sites other contractors can’t reach.

Each area finished as soon as it’s released, including the steep, soft and remote ground where truck-mounted units stop.

Request a Quote Truck or track?
The outcome

Get in, finish the job, get out.

High-capacity FINN T330 hydroseeders and support equipment cover more ground per load and per day. Fewer trips means your area is seeded and off the punch list sooner, and that saves you time and money.

  • High-capacity FINN T330 hydroseeders with the support equipment to keep them running.
  • Native seed mixes, mulch, tackifier and fertilizer tailored to your site and permit requirements.
  • Self-sufficient on remote sites. Crews can pump water from on-site sources such as streams or ponds when there’s no hydrant or water-truck access.
The capability

Truck or track: the right machine for every slope.

Track-mounted equipment reaches steep slopes, soft ground and remote sites that truck-mounted units can’t. We run both and match the machine to the area.

Site conditionTruck-mountedTrack-mounted
Paved or graded access roads✓ Best fit, fastest between areasCapable
Shoulders, medians, flat pads✓ Best fitCapable
Steep slopesLimited reach✓ Best fit, gets on and along the slope
Soft, wet or freshly placed groundRisk of rutting or getting stuck✓ Best fit, low ground pressure
Remote sites without roadsNot practical✓ Best fit
Rough terrain (ski runs, mine benches)Not practical✓ Best fit

Some areas are too steep or unstable for any ground equipment. For those, we use drone seeding or aerial seeding.

Where it applies

Built for the GC workflow.

We know we’re the last contractor in. We work area by area as sections are released, so finished ground gets stabilized right away instead of waiting on the whole site.

  • Highway, road and interchange construction
  • Pipeline, transmission and utility corridors
  • Mine reclamation and overburden slopes
  • Ski resort and mountain development terrain
  • Commercial and residential site development
  • Post-fire and post-disturbance stabilization on public land
