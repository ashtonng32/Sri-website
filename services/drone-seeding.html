The outcome

Coverage on dangerous ground, without putting people on it.

Steep cut slopes, loose overburden, unstable fill and remote drainages are where reclamation schedules stall and safety exposure climbs. Drone application takes the crew off the slope entirely. Seed and herbicide go exactly where the plan calls for them, and the ground you just stabilized stays undisturbed.

  • Precise application patterns. Programmed flight paths and rates put seed and product where the spec calls for it.
  • Less ground disturbance. No tracks, no foot traffic, no ruts on freshly graded or seeded surfaces.
  • Fast coverage. Large areas treated quickly, so released sections don’t sit bare.
  • Safer on steep or unstable ground. Pilots stay at a safe launch point, away from the hazard.

Basic Aviation Risk Standard

Cleared to fly where safety requirements are strictest.

SRI is a BARS Silver–certified drone operator under the Flight Safety Foundation’s Basic Aviation Risk Standard. Major mining and resource companies use this aviation safety standard to vet the aircraft operators they contract.

What BARS Silver means for you

BARS certification comes from an independent audit, not a checklist we filled out ourselves. In practical terms:

Audited safety system

An independently audited safety management system governs how we plan, fly and report.

Documented risk controls

Hazards are identified and controlled in writing for each operation, so your safety team can review exactly what we’ll do.

Qualified pilots

Pilots are trained, qualified and current, with records to prove it.

Maintained aircraft

Aircraft maintenance is tracked and recorded, and every flight starts with a serviceable machine.

Faster approval

At sites that require BARS, we can be approved quickly. You don’t have to vet a new aviation vendor from scratch.
