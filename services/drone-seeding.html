Drone seeding and herbicide application

Seed and herbicide on steep, unstable or remote ground with no crew on the slope. DJI Agras T100, T50 and T40 aircraft, flown by a BARS Silver operator.

Coverage on ground no one should walk

Steep cut slopes, loose overburden and remote drainages are where schedules stall. A drone takes the crew off the slope entirely.

  • Programmed application patterns

    Flight paths and rates put seed and product where the spec calls for them.

  • Less ground disturbance

    No tracks, no foot traffic, no ruts on freshly graded or seeded surfaces.

  • Fast coverage

    Large areas treated quickly, so released sections do not sit bare.

  • Pilots away from the hazard

    The crew works from a safe launch point, never on the unstable ground.

What BARS Silver means for your site

The Flight Safety Foundation’s Basic Aviation Risk Standard is how mining and resource companies vet aircraft operators. Our Silver certification comes from an independent audit.

What the audit covers

  • Safety management system

    Independently audited planning, flying and reporting.

  • Documented risk controls

    Hazards controlled in writing for each operation, for your safety team to review.

  • Qualified pilots

    Trained, qualified and current, with records to show it.
