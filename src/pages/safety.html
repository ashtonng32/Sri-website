Basic Aviation Risk Standard

BARS Silver–certified drone operations.

The Flight Safety Foundation’s Basic Aviation Risk Standard is the aviation safety standard major mining and resource companies use to vet contracted aircraft operators. SRI holds BARS Silver certification, which comes from an independent audit of our operations. At sites that require BARS, that means a faster approval.
