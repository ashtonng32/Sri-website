Our story

Three decades on Utah’s hardest ground.

Skyline Reclamation started in 1995 in Fairview, a small town in Sanpete County. Since then we’ve grown into one of Utah’s most efficient and effective native-lands revegetation contractors, without losing the family-run approach we started with.

Our work has kept pace with the job. We added track-mounted equipment for terrain trucks can’t reach, high-capacity hydroseeders to finish areas faster, and a BARS Silver–certified drone program for ground no crew should be standing on.
