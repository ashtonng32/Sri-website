The outcome

Defensible space, thinned stands, usable ground.

Mechanical mulching turns brush and small timber into mulch in place. Fuel goes down, the soil is protected, and there’s no burning or hauling.

  • Reduced wildfire fuel loads around structures, communities and infrastructure.
  • Wildland-urban interface work where fire risk meets homes and facilities.
  • Timber thinning and clearing on public and private land.
  • Mulch left in place to protect soil and limit erosion.
