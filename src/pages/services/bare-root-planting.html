The outcome

Shrubs and trees in the ground and on their way.

Seeding establishes grasses and forbs. Planting establishes the shrubs and trees that take years to come in on their own. Our crews plant at volume on remote and rough sites, to agency and permit specifications.

  • Native bare-root and container stock planted to spec.
  • Volume crews that plant about 40,000 plants a year.
  • Public-land experience with BLM, National Forest, DWR and SITLA projects.
