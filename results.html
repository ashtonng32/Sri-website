Four jobs, four hard parts

The challenge, how we handled it, and what the client got.

  1. Mayflower Mountain Resort: ski-run slopes

    Runs too steep and rough for standard rigs. Track-mounted hydroseeding and mulching equipment worked directly on the slopes.

  2. Rio Tinto Kennecott: cleared to fly

    An active mine that vets every aircraft operator. BARS Silver drone seeding with documented risk controls, for a client that brings us back each year.

  3. Deer Creek Mine for PacifiCorp

    Returning a closed mine site to native vegetation.

  4. High country: work above 9,000 feet

    Remote, high-elevation terrain with no practical ground access, seeded and mulched from the air.
